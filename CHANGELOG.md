# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- Settings changed on the Mod Manager's Mods screen take effect at once, except Rotate Enabled and MouseButton, Zoom ExtraIn and ExtraOut, and the seven keys in Keys other than CenterKeyResetsView, which take effect at the next game start. A changed setting keeps the view you turned to.
- The Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer.

## 0.3.1

First public release. A free camera for Survival Log: orbit all the way round, tilt, raise and lower, zoom further, and no more snapping back to your character unless you press the center camera key.
